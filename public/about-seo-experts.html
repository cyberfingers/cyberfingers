Working principles

Useful work has a point of view.

These principles keep the engagement focused on durable improvements rather than vanity scores.

01 / Evidence

Data before assumptions

Search Console, analytics, crawl behavior, and the actual rendered pages provide the starting point.

02 / Clarity

Explain the “why”

Recommendations include the reason, likely impact, dependencies, and the tradeoffs a team should understand.

03 / Focus

Priorities over volume

A short list of well-ordered improvements is more valuable than a long export of everything a tool can detect.
