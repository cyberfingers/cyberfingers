Measure what changes

Search data is a feedback loop.

Analytics and Search Console are used to establish a baseline, identify patterns, and see how the site responds after improvements ship.

Rankings can fluctuate and no consultant can guarantee a specific position. The focus is on building a technically sound, useful site and measuring the signals that show progress.
